### Resurrection Catholic Missions: Digital Transformation and Strategic Roadmap

#### Executive Summary

This briefing document outlines a comprehensive six-month digital transformation strategy for Resurrection Catholic Missions (RCM) of the South, a Black Catholic parish in Montgomery, Alabama. The project aims to modernize the mission’s digital presence, stabilize communication infrastructure, and address critical operational challenges—most notably an all-time low in school enrollment.The strategy moves through nine distinct phases, transitioning from foundation-setting and technical remediation to audience capture and automated content production. Success is defined not merely by aesthetic improvements, but by the establishment of a self-sustaining system that the parish can operate independently. Key objectives include reducing the mobile bounce rate (the primary technical complaint), increasing completed donations, and generating school enrollment inquiries.

#### Project Vision and Objectives

RCM is a unique institution intersecting food security, K-8 education, disability services, and racial justice. The project is led by an alumnus (V) whose goal is to provide high-leverage technical and communications work that leaves the mission better than it was found.

##### Core Outcomes

1. **Organizational Independence:**  Establish a working mobile site, owned email lists, and active content channels that a trained parish editor can manage without external assistance.  
2. **Documented Proof of Work:**  Generate a numbers-backed case study proving the efficacy of the digital transformation.  
3. **Reusable Assets:**  Develop a library of templates (content audits, AI workflows, brand kits) that can be utilized for future institutional growth.

##### Operational Principles

* **Absolute Source Fidelity:**  Every claim of improvement must be backed by a baseline captured in Phase 1\.  
* **Sequencing Discipline:**  The workflow follows a "Read-only audit → backup confirmed → human sign-off → action" hierarchy.  
* **Facitilitation over Authorship:**  Digital channels will amplify existing voices (such as Fr. Manuel Williams) rather than inventing new positions.

#### Performance Metrics and KPIs

Progress is measured through "The one number that matters most":  **Mobile Bounce Rate.**  This was the original technical complaint and is the most legible indicator of success for a non-technical audience.

##### Client Results (RCM)

* **Website:**  Total sessions, mobile vs. desktop share, and giving-link click-through rates.  
* **Search:**  Ranking for local queries such as "food bank Montgomery" and "Catholic school Montgomery."  
* **Owned Audience:**  Email subscribers and contacts captured per event (net new metrics).  
* **Social:**  Engagement rates and reach on repurposed vs. original content.  
* **Programs:**   **School enrollment inquiries**  (the primary institutional objective), food bank requests, and volunteer sign-ups.

##### Internal Assets

* **Documentation:**  Weekly build log entries and published case studies.  
* **Reusable Tooling:**  AI workflow audit templates, brand kit processes, and a transferable "content machine."

#### Strategic Challenges and Governance

##### School Integration and Liability

A significant operational gap exists regarding the school's digital presence. Currently, school content and photos of minors are posted via a principal's personal Facebook account. This presents two issues:

1. **Liability:**  Potential violation of diocesan child protection and media release policies.  
2. **Missed Opportunity:**  Content does not reach the broader parish audience.**The Fix:**  Transition the school to a dedicated subsite and parish-owned social accounts. This must be framed to the principal as "giving her a better tool" rather than reducing her control.

##### Leadership and Vision

The organization currently lacks a shared vision, leading to multiple parties interpreting direction independently. Unused technological assets (Chromebooks, gaming chairs, Roombas) and a dismantled STEM program highlight the need for Fr. Manuel Williams to set a clear, centralized vision that the team can then implement via defined milestones.

##### Ethical Use of AI

* **Voice Cloning:**  While AI narration of Fr. Williams’ writings is technically possible, it will only be pursued with explicit, informed consent and clear audience disclosure to maintain trust.  
* **Grant Writing:**  AI-assisted grant writing is identified as a major untapped opportunity to secure funding for the food bank, school, and disability care.

#### Technical and Operational Decisions

##### Infrastructure and Tooling

* **Project Management:**   **ClickUp**  was selected over Notion and Google Sheets due to its superior API access, Kanban/Gantt views, and cross-platform compatibility (Windows/Mobile).  
* **Site Development:**  The project will focus on a  **responsive retrofit**  rather than a full redesign to lower risk and address the immediate mobile rendering complaints without unnecessary visual overhauls.  
* **Documentation:**  A single "raw" build log will be maintained, with redactions occurring at the time of publication rather than capture to ensure efficiency.

##### Financial and Tax Realities

A critical correction was noted regarding the tax treatment of donated work:  **Professional services are not tax-deductible.**  RCM and the project lead will not plan around "forgone income" or market-value deductions. Only actual out-of-pocket expenses (hosting, software, mileage) will be tracked for tax purposes.

##### Content Strategy (The "Manual Soak")

Automation of content will be deferred until after a six-week manual posting period (Nov–Dec). This "soak period" is essential to identify what content resonates with the three target tracks:

* **Elders/Donors:**  Facebook (Primary weight).  
* **Working Parents:**  Instagram.  
* **Youth:**  TikTok/Reels.
