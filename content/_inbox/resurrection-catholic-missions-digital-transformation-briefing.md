### Resurrection Catholic Missions Digital Transformation Briefing

#### Executive Summary

This document synthesizes the strategic roadmap for the digital transformation of Resurrection Catholic Missions of the South (RCM), a Black Catholic parish in Montgomery, Alabama. The project aims to modernize the institution’s digital presence, stabilize communication infrastructure, and reverse a critical decline in school enrollment.The strategy follows a rigorous eight-phase timeline spanning September 2026 to March 2027\. Key objectives include a mobile-responsive website retrofit, the establishment of "owned" audience channels (CRM/Email), and the implementation of a content engine that repurposes liturgical material for three distinct demographic tracks. Success is defined not by visual aesthetics, but by a self-sustaining system that remains operational after the project lead’s departure, evidenced by improved mobile bounce rates and increased donations.

#### Project Context and Institutional Significance

Resurrection Catholic Missions is a multi-faceted organization operating at the intersection of food security, education, disability services, and racial justice. It currently manages:

* **A K-8 School:**  Currently facing an all-time low in enrollment.  
* **A Food Bank:**  Providing essential community services.  
* **Disability Services:**  Care for mentally handicapped children and adults.  
* **Justice and Peace Work:**  A public commitment to racial justice with a historical Black Lives Matter position.The digital transformation is led by an alumnus (Vijay) whose goal is to provide high-value technical remediation while building a documented, numbers-backed case study for future nonprofit applications.

#### Performance Metrics (KPIs)

Progress is measured through two distinct sets of metrics: Client Results (RCM) and Project Assets (Mine).

##### The Primary Objective: Mobile Bounce Rate

The "one number that matters most" is the mobile bounce rate. This was the catalyst for the project and represents a legible, attributable metric for non-technical stakeholders.

##### Secondary Client KPIs

* **Website/Search:**  Total sessions, giving-link click-through rate, and Google Business Profile views/calls.  
* **Financials:**  Count and total of completed donations.  
* **Programs:**  School enrollment inquiries (the "real" objective), food bank appointments, and volunteer sign-ups.  
* **Audience:**  Email subscriber growth and engagement rates (social reach).

##### Project Assets (Compounding Value)

* **Documentation:**  Build log entries, case studies, and a "friction log."  
* **Reusable Tooling:**  Content and AI workflow audit templates, brand kit processes, and training course materials.

#### School Revitalization Strategy

The school is in an urgent state due to record-low enrollment and a lack of structured marketing.

##### Addressing Organizational Challenges

* **Governance Gap:**  School content currently resides on a principal’s personal Facebook account. This presents a liability (minor consent) and a missed opportunity for parish-wide reach. The roadmap moves this to a parish-owned account.  
* **Resource Utilization:**  Significant donated technology (Chromebooks, gaming chairs, Roombas) is currently sitting unused.  
* **Content Plan:**  A short-form video strategy (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) focusing on exercises like Tai chi, calisthenics, and kickboxing to engage youth.  
* **STEM Integration:**  A proposed "train-the-teacher" program where students eventually assist in content production as a media skills initiative.

##### Funding and Retention

The "Choose Act" provides $7,000 per student, but the school lacks a retention clause to prevent parents from taking funds elsewhere. Grant writing is identified as a major untapped opportunity, with plans to build an AI-assisted grant pipeline.

#### Technical and Operational Principles

##### 1\. Decision Log and Tooling

* **Project Tracking:**  ClickUp was selected over Notion and Google Sheets due to its API accessibility and native Gantt/Kanban views.  
* **The Build Log:**  A single running log (raw capture) serves as the source of truth, to be redacted only at the time of public sharing.  
* **Site Approach:**  A "responsive retrofit" (CSS/breakpoints) was chosen over a full redesign to lower risk and focus on the functional mobile complaint.

##### 2\. Content Facilitation vs. Authorship

The project lead acts as an amplifier for leadership, not an author.

* **The "Manual Soak":**  Content must be posted manually for six weeks (Nov–Dec) to understand what resonates before building automation tools.  
* **AI Ethics:**  AI voice cloning for Father Manuel’s writings is deferred, requiring explicit consent and clear audience disclosure to maintain trust.

##### 3\. Safety and Sequencing

* **Absolute Source Fidelity:**  No change is made without a baseline captured first.  
* **Read-Only First:**  The workflow follows: Audit → Backup → Sign-off → Action.  
* **Privacy:**  A "human-first whitelist" model is used for public documentation. An AI agent never decides what is public; it only assists in redacting what a human has flagged as sensitive.

##### 4\. Financial Clarification

Professional services donated to the parish are  **not**  tax-deductible under IRS rules. Only actual out-of-pocket expenses (software, hosting, mileage) can be deducted. The project is managed with this understanding, focusing on the credibility and portfolio value of the work rather than tax offsets.

#### Definition of Success at Six Months

Success is reached when the parish can operate its communications independently. This requires:

1. A working mobile site and functional online giving links.  
2. An owned email list and three active content channels.  
3. A trained editor and a named system owner.  
4. A documented case study proving the digital transformation resulted in measurable growth.
